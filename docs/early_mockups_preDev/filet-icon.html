Filet — icon candidates
108dp canvas · dashed ring = 72dp safe zone · masks: circle / squircle / rounded

What the icon has to survive

  • Adaptive icon geometry. 108dp canvas, but only the centre 72dp is guaranteed visible — every OEM applies its own mask. Anything outside the dashed ring can be cropped.
  • 48px in a launcher is the real viewing size. Anything that needs detail to read is already wrong.
  • A monochrome layer (Android 13+ themed icons) — one flat silhouette, no colour, no gradient. If the idea only works in two tones, it fails here.
  • 24px in the status bar as a notification glyph.

The idea being tested

Filet = a clean cut that opens something and shows what is inside. The app's own signature is the dual pane — a vertical division. Those are the same shape, which is why every candidate below is some version of a folder with a cut through it. The cut is the name, the product, and the layout, in one mark.

Deliberately not used: the handwritten N. That is Nic's maker's mark and belongs on the About screen and the F-Droid author field, not on the product — the workshop has a name, the craftsman stamps his own mark on what leaves it.
