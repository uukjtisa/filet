The tabs along the top

A tab shows the icon of what it's open on: a tab for a place like Home or Playlists shows that tab's icon, and a folder tab shows that folder's own icon, emblem included. A home-screen shortcut to a folder uses the same picture as its row, and follows it when you change it.
