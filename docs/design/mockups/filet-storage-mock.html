Disk map

The file list as a disk map: same rows, same actions, ordered by what they take.

View

Walk

Decisions, in order.

1. A mode of the file list, not a page. Turned on from the view menu's Disk map (in the folder being shown) or a drive card's hold menu (at the drive's top). It stays on while you walk in and out of folders, until the × on its strip.

2. One walk per drive measures every folder on the way, so going into a folder shows its sizes at once. Rows show a floor with + until the walk has reached them, and are never shown as final before it has.

3. Largest first. The bar under each name is its share of the folder it is in; the strip's bar is the drive.

4. Three views from the same walk: Folders (the tree), Largest files (the biggest files anywhere below, flat), By type (what kinds take the space).

5. Every action the list has works here: select, delete, move, copy, share. A delete takes its size off every folder above it at once.

6. Every drive Filet opens, network shares included. On a share the strip says the walk reads every folder over the network, and Stop is always there.

7. Nothing is deleted or suggested automatically. It shows; you choose.
