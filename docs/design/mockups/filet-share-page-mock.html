What changed
  • Shared files first. Under the card, not after Devices. The section header has Select to stop several at once.
  • A row per file: the name others see, with the real name under it if you renamed it, then size, lock, "stops in 3 h", and downloads with when the last one was. The QR button is at the end.
  • Hold or right-click for: Show QR and link, Rename for others, Expiry, Pause, One-time link, Lock, Stop sharing.
  • A folder says it downloads as a zip in a browser.
  • The card's settings fold into one line that says how they're set, so the list shows without scrolling. Tap it to change them.
  • Require a PIN stays as it is today. The lock is an extra lock on one file, on top of the access code, for things others shouldn't get. A locked file asks for its own lock code, shown on the card under the access code.
  • Devices starts with Pop-ups: On, Off for 1 hour, Off. It covers every phone, trusted ones too. Off for 1 hour turns itself back on.
  • While pop-ups are off, a phone sending you something shows here as sharing. Tap it to see what it's sending. It leaves the list within about 30 s of the sender cancelling, leaving the Wi-Fi or closing Filet, and after 10 minutes at the latest.
  • Each phone says if it sends without asking, and has Block. A blocked phone's pushes are turned away without a pop-up, and Activity logs them.
  • Pushes arrive whenever Filet is open, not only while sharing. Turn Sharing off at the top and send again.
  • Received files, Let in and Connected are unchanged.

5 · The share in Activity

Everything the share does is logged on the Activity tab, in ActivitySheet.kt's rows: downloads by others, pushes sent and received, and shares started, paused and stopped. Do things elsewhere on this page and they land here. The started card and the end message stay switchable in Settings › Activity.
