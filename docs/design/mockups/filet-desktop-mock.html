Round 1, 2026-11-12

Current (replica) is today's window, copied from FiletWindow.kt and Kit.kt: Ink palette (#F7F6F2 bg, #2F6B4F accent, #9A4A1C warn), 620×760, 10px card radius, 6px buttons, every button inline. Warts kept on purpose: one long column, rows of five equal-weight buttons, the mount form unfolding inside the list, the drive-letter stepper, free space as a sentence.

Dark dashboard (proposal):

  • A status strip on top answers "is it working" at a glance: sharing on/off and folder count, this PC's address, phones online and drives mounted. Autostart moves up there as a switch.
  • Two columns: what this PC offers on the left, phones on the right. No scrolling between the two halves.
  • Colours come from the Filet mark: #4E7382 primary, #7FA8B8 accent, on #0E1315 / #151C1F panels. State pills: green mounted, grey not mounted, dim offline, amber for a drive whose phone isn't there.
  • Each phone is a tile: a used/free bar from the phone's own figures, one primary action, the rest secondary.
  • Mount is a dialog, not a form inside the list. The letter picker is a chip row of every letter it may take. Amber chips say "replace X: (name)". Letters held by non-Filet drives never appear.

Try it: the Mount button on the LGN-LX2 tile or "Open the mount dialog" up top. Pick a letter, type any code, press Mount. Not real code: nothing here talks to a phone.
