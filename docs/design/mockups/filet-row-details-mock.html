Today

As shipped. The warts are kept on purpose; the numbers below match the fixes on the right.

  1. The preview isn't a row. Two lines of monospace text: no icon, a different font from the list, 12 sp where List draws 13 and Comfortable 14, and folders in the same weight as files.
  2. It shows a line your list may not have. The line under the name is drawn only in Comfortable view. List (the default) and Compact draw one line per row, so every control in the top half changes nothing you can see there.
  3. Relative dates can't be seen working. The sample is 12 days old, and relative dates stop at 6, so the switch never changes the preview. Its hint also promises "Today, Yesterday and weekday names", while the list writes today, yesterday, 3 days ago.
  4. Two lists for one line. What's on the line and what isn't sit in two lists under a 9 sp header, and no row says what it prints or whether it's for files or folders (that's behind a hold).
  5. Eight controls with no grouping. The section has no sentence under its title, and the side columns and the date format run on from the line builder with nothing to tell them apart.
  6. The date chips are names, not dates. "All numbers / Named month / Full month" means picking a format without seeing it. A named month also keeps the leading zero: 02 Nov 2026.
  7. "Dates show only in wide panes" is only on hold, so choosing Date beside a file and seeing nothing in two panes looks broken.

Proposed

Same settings and the same stored values, plus one new one: Time of day.

  1. The preview is the list. Five rows drawn as the list draws them: the drawn icons, the list's font and sizes, folder names in medium weight, the right-hand column lined up with even-width digits. It's built from the same functions the list calls, so it can't drift.
  2. It follows your view. It starts in the view your lists use, and the switch on it shows the other two. When the line under the name is hidden in that view, it says so, with a link to switch to Comfortable.
  3. Dates of every age: today, yesterday, 3 days, 12 days and 6 weeks back. Relative dates and Time of day both visibly change something. The hint now says what the list actually writes.
  4. One list of parts: a tick box turns each on or off, arrows move the ones that are on, and each part shows what it prints on the preview and whether it's for files or folders, without a hold.
  5. Three groups: Under the name · Beside the name · Dates, and one sentence under the title.
  6. Date chips show the date. They follow your order and separator. A named month drops the leading zero: 2 Nov 2026. Numbers keep it: 2026-11-02.
  7. The wide-pane rule is printed under Beside the name whenever Date is chosen there.
  8. Time of day NEW: Off · 14:32 · 2:32 PM, joined to the date by a dot: 2 Nov 2026 · 14:32. It's the last-modified time, beside the date wherever a date is printed (the line and the right-hand column). The column widens to fit it, so it still lines up.
