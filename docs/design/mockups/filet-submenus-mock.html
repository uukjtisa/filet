How a submenu behaves

  • Where it opens. Beside the menu, its top level with the row that opened it, overlapping the menu's edge by 4 dp so the two read as one. Right of the menu when there is room, left of it when there is not.
  • Never off the screen. With no room on either side it is pushed in from the screen edge and covers part of the menu it came from. It moves up rather than run off the bottom. Taller than the screen, it scrolls.
  • Closing. Its own back row closes it. Back closes the newest submenu first, then the menu. Tapping the row that opened it closes it again. Tapping outside closes everything.
  • Nesting. A row inside a submenu can open another, by the same rules against the submenu it came from. Only one submenu is open per level: opening another row's submenu closes the first.
  • The open row is lit in the menu below it, so it is clear what the submenu belongs to while it covers part of the menu.

One Share

  • The hold menu and the selection bar's More card get one Share row in place of Nearby and Fast share. Its submenu has two sections: Over the internet (Filet Portal) and Nearby (Fast share, Add to nearby share).
  • Send, which hands files to another app, stays where it is: it is in the hold menu's top row and is not one of Filet's own ways.
  • The selection bar can carry Share as a button. It opens the same two sections as Filet's standard dialogue, since a bar at the bottom of the screen has no menu to sit beside.
  • A bar that was set up with Nearby or Fast share on it shows Share in that place instead.
