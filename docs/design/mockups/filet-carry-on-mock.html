What today's page does that the new one fixes:

  • The two filters are two rows of chips, each three chips wide, so the right half of both rows is empty.
  • The first chip row sits right on the header's rule with no space above it.
  • The title row holds only the title. The count is further down, on a heading of its own.
  • Forget everything is at the very bottom, after the last row. It clears everything at once, with no question and no Undo.
  • The percentage pill is not at the right edge. It sits about three-quarters across, so pills on rows with short titles float in the middle.
  • Select only exists inside a row's hold menu.

The new page:

  • One header: the title with "9 part way · 4 finished" under it, and Forget all at the top right.
  • The filters are two drop-downs on one row, each listing its counts. A filter moved off its default is lit. Select uses the rest of that row. While selecting, the same row becomes ✕ · N selected · All · Forget, so nothing below it moves.
  • Optional: the last thing played as a large card with Carry on and Start over.
  • Optional: rows grouped by day, with the same headings New files uses.
  • Each row shows its percentage and time left at the right edge. How far in is drawn along the bottom of the picture, and the pictures are a little larger.
