Cards and messages · round 3

Filet tells you about work twice: a started card top right the moment a job begins (StartedCards.kt), and a finished message at the bottom when it ends (the Toast in BrowserScreen). This is the page that lets you choose both, per group (copy and move, archives, delete, network…) with each action's own switch under it, plus how long a card stays, where it sits and how many stack. Not the system notifications: those stay in Android's settings. Everything here works; every name is a proposal waiting on your OK.

2 · Try it: start some work

Each button starts a pretend job with the words the real job would use. The card follows your Starts switch and the three card settings, and the message follows the Ends switch. A tap on a card would open Activity at that job, so it closes the card here. Start several at once to see the stack, and one more than it holds to see the oldest pushed out early.

3 · Where a card can sit

The four places, each with two cards and a finished message. At the top, cards hang under the status bar as they do today, newest at the top. At the bottom, they stack upward from just above the finished message, newest at the bottom, so a card never covers the message or the bar. Cards slide in from their own side. The current choice is ringed.

4 · The one list every switch comes from

The page is built from JobKind, the list that already gives every job its Activity icon. Each kind gets its group, its name and whether it posts a finished message in one exhaustive when, so a new kind doesn't compile until it has a place here, and the page draws one row per kind. The setting keeps only what you turned off, so a new kind arrives switched on, like everything today.



  
Rules the build must keep

  
    
    
  • One list. Groups, rows and switches are drawn from JobKind.entries, never typed out. A test walks every kind and checks it has a group, a name, and a row on the page. A group with one action shows as one row with no caret.
    • 
    
  • Off is what's stored. One key, notices.v1, holds the kinds whose card or message you turned off, plus the three card settings. Unknown names in it are skipped, so a file from a newer Filet still loads. It travels with Export like the other look-and-feel settings.
    • 
    
  • A switch only where there's something to switch. A kind that posts no finished message today shows a dash in Ends, not a switch that does nothing.
    • 
    
  • Activity still lists everything. These switches only decide what pops up. Every job is still a row in Activity, and Keep finished jobs works as it does now.
    • 
    
  • A refusal still gets its dialogue. When Android or a server refuses (the Denial dialogue), that is not a finished message and none of these switches hide it.
    • 
    
  • The stack holds what you chose. One more card than "Stacks up to" pushes the oldest out early, as the fourth does today. A card still leaves on its own timer, even with system animations off.
    • 
    
  • Messages that aren't about a job are untouched: "Path copied", "Renamed", "Select something first", every one of those keeps showing.
    • 
    
  • A failure always shows. A failed job's message ignores its Ends switch. A switch that stops "3 copied" never hides that nothing was copied.
    • 
    
  • Stays for is the cards' only. The finished message keeps its 1.9 s.
    • 
    
  • Default: everything on, 3 s, top right, 3 at once. That's today's app.
    •
