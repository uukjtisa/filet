Why a corner button and not a rearrangeable side bar. The bottom of this screen already carries the nav bar, the now-playing strip, the selection bar and the paste pill. A fifth draggable band would cover the list permanently and has to collide-detect against four others. A round button in a corner moves out of the way by being small, and it can still be dragged left or right if the right corner is wrong for your thumb.

The badge is the point. With nothing copied the dock is just +. With something on the clipboard it carries a count you cannot walk past, which is the actual fault today: after Copy the app holds state that nothing on screen mentions.

The paste pill stays. It is already per-pane and already knows which folder "here" means. With one batch it reads Paste 7 here exactly as now; with several it reads Paste 3 batches here and opens the tray on a long press. The dock is the way in when nothing is copied; the pill is the way in when something is.

3 · The music player — beautified

Artwork is the real poster frame out of the clip you tested with, so the ambient colour is what Filet would actually derive from it. A is what ships today. B, C and D are the revamp at three levels of commitment. Tap the big button to pause — the art reacts. Tap the queue handle at the bottom.

What changed, newest first

Round 4 — the marquee fades at both ends and only runs when the title does not fit, measured rather than guessed. C's title no longer crosses the artwork: its wrapper had flex:1 in a block parent, which does nothing, so it sized to its own text and clipped nothing. D is rebuilt twice over — blurred fill under a contained sharp copy so nothing is stretched, and small retractable controls instead of the glass panel, because a panel across the bottom defeats the one thing that face is for. The wash is wider and fainter. And the controls take their colour from the artwork, sampled weighted towards the pixels that carry colour and then lightened until it clears 4.5:1 against the panel, with the glyph going black or white by whichever wins.DECIDED

Round 3 — you pick which files to paste, not the whole batch: every file in an open batch has a tick and the button counts them, so Paste and Paste 4 are one control at two states. The × still removes a file for good, which is a different intention. On the player: the ambient light is three layers instead of one, because one blurred copy of a nearly black clip is a dark rectangle; the title scrolls, since a filename is long and cannot wrap; and Uninterrupted now sits on a chip row under the transport in the title bar, lit, with the word in the eyebrow when it is on. B chosen, D rebuilt rather than dropped.

Round 2 — the music player, four faces, on the real poster frame. The clipboard tray opens each batch to its individual files, with a size and an × per file: a count you cannot open is all there is. Its destination is stated as a folder name rather than a raw path, because the raw one was rendering back to front. The selection bar is settled at C.

Round 1 — four selection-bar heights against the real list, and the dock with a clipboard tray built from the batch model in docs/design/clipboard-tray.md. Nothing is in the app yet.
