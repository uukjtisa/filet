Round 1

  • Name card, A0 is the current one. Reproduced exactly — the 42%-of-the-card white sheen, the plain rule, 27px system sans. Compare it against your phone before judging the others; if A0 here does not match the app, the mock is wrong and I want to know.
  • What Trawl actually has that Filet does not, having read both: an engraved flourish instead of a 1px bar, a display serif, a sheen tinted with the accent and sized to the name rather than the card, a bigger eyebrow mark, and a touch-triggered sweep. A1 brings all five across.
  • Support card moved under Project, and shrunk. B2 is the pick: it reads as the second thing on the page without becoming a second header competing with your name.
  • Trawl row relabelled to "A recommended multi-platform media downloader".
  • A4 exists because the font is a real decision. Fraunces is 130 KB. I think it is worth it — the serif is most of why Trawl's card reads as designed rather than assembled — but a no-new-asset option should be on the table rather than assumed away.
