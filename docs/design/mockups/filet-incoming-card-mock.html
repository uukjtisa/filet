Show over other appsGranted once in Android's settings. Without it, a send asks with a notification.
  • Side card slides in from the right edge under the status bar, over the app in use. The rest of the screen stays usable. Tapping a thumbnail views that file; "Open in full" turns the card into the full dialog.
  • Mini card is fast share's: one line, one thumbnail, Download or dismiss.
  • Full dialog (optional, the "How a send asks" setting) sits in the centre with the preview: pictures shown, video and audio playable (muted at first when that is on), text and Markdown readable. Any other file shows its type icon. Swipe the stage or tap the strip to move between files.
  • Viewing before accepting streams from the sender and saves nothing. Accept saves to Filet/Received, and after that View opens the saved copy.
  • View opens full screen over the app (Back returns to it) or in a floating window that can be dragged and resized, with a video still playing while the app underneath is used.
  • New, 2026-11-15: one design, one pipeline. Inside Filet the send asks with the same side card, mini card or full dialog as over another app, previews included, drawn over Filet's own screen. Today's in-app dialogue goes. Whatever "How a send asks" says holds everywhere; the notification is used only when Filet is closed and cannot draw over other apps.
  • New, 2026-11-15: the settings live in Settings › Activity, as a Nearby group under Cards and messages, beside the other things that pop up. Pop-ups moves there from the Share page too, so every option about how a send asks sits in one place. The Share page keeps a single row that opens this group. Chosen over the first proposal, a Settings tab of its own: five options do not earn a tab, and Activity already owns "what pops up and when".
