Home edit state

A visible Edit button, and editing the real Home in place

Try in the edit state

Round 4 - no control in the header. The pencil beside search looked out of place, so the header is as it was. The way in is a hold: on any empty part of Home, or on a card's heading, whose menu now opens with Edit Home. In this mock, hold the empty space below the last card.

Round 3 - a quieter way in. The word Edit made the button too wide beside search. Now it's a pencil the same size as the search glyph, beside it. Holding any empty part of Home opens the edit state too; holding a file, a drive or a card's heading keeps its own menu.

Round 2 - the drives move too. In the edit state every drive can be moved among the others: hold it and drag, and the drives it passes slide out of its way. A grip in front of its icon (where the card strips keep their handle too) says it moves; the x is not drawn while editing, since the eye does that job. Grouped by kind, a drive moves within its own group. The order is saved with the rest and comes back with Import; a drive plugged in for the first time joins at the end.

Today. The only way in is to hold a card's heading and pick Rearrange. Then every card disappears and a list of names takes its place, so you're arranging words, not Home. Only the two file feeds have options, under their names, with a small preview because the real card isn't on screen.

The way in. A hold on any empty part of Home, or Edit Home in the menu a held card heading opens (round 4; rounds 1 to 3 had a button in the header).

Editing. Home stays Home: every card drawn in full with its real contents, live. Each card gets a strip on top with a drag handle, its name, Options, up and down arrows, and an eye to hide it. Drag by the handle or tap the arrows; the card lifts and the others slide out of its way. Every change shows at once on the real card below.

Options, per card. Storage: one row of cards or grouped by kind, a card per folder or one per app, and show or hide capacity bars. Each drive tile gets its own eye to take just that drive off Home. New files and Opened: how many rows, the density, and same density for both. Categories: which categories show, and list or gallery for photos, videos and audio.

Hidden cards collect in a tray at the end, one line each with Show, so nothing hidden is out of reach.

Saving. Every change is saved as it's made, into the same settings as the rest: Export carries Home's layout under Appearance and layout, and Import brings it back. Reset puts Home back to its first arrangement. Done or Back leaves.

Decided as drawn; the header control went in round 4.
