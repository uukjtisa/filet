Round 1 (2026-11-13)

Dock. A new Copy or Move shows the Paste pill, as today. After the first paste it shrinks into a 42dp clipboard circle, the size of a dock leaf, with a badge counting what is held. The next new Copy or Move brings the pill back. Tapping the pill pastes the new items here. The pill's arrow and the circle both open the clipboard.

Clipboard. It's one list in two groups. New holds things not pasted yet; History holds things already pasted once. Every row has a one-tap button that does its job here, Paste for a copy and Move for a move. A moved item stays in History and remembers where it is now, so you can move it again and again. ⋯ on a row offers Copy here instead, Show the files, Open where it is, and Remove. Select switches the rows to ticks for doing several at once.

Action cards. Every job that shows in Activity gets a card at the top right the moment it starts, naming it and where it goes. The cards stack, newest on top. Each one lasts 3 seconds, with a hairline showing the time left. Tapping a card opens Activity at that job. The bottom message when a job finishes stays as it is.

Player. Filet's player becomes the default. The video player's menu gets a PLAYER group with two rows. Tapping one switches on the spot, at the same moment, and keeps the choice for every video after. The same choice moves from Developer into Settings › Video.

Defaults I picked; say if any is wrong.
1. History survives closing Filet. It keeps the last 30 entries and drops the oldest.
2. A History row whose files are gone (deleted elsewhere) stays, greyed, with Remove.
3. The ⋯ menu's "Copy here instead" on a move row copies and leaves the files where they are.
